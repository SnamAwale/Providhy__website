Nepal-Specific

Built for Nepali Billing

Features designed specifically for how businesses sell in Nepal

Sales Register

Automatically generate a detailed, IRD-compliant sales register. Perfect for your official records and tax filing.

Automatic VAT

13% VAT automatically calculated and applied. Support for both inclusive and exclusive pricing.

Item-wise Tax Control

Apply tax on an item-by-item basis. This ensures accurate VAT totals even on invoices with mixed taxable and exempt goods.

Invoice Numbering

IRD-compliant sequential invoice numbering with fiscal year prefix. No gaps, no duplicates.

Nepali Calendar

Full Bikram Sambat support. View sales reports and invoice dates in Nepali calendar format.

Fiscal Year Reporting

All reports, summaries, and numbering are built around Nepal's fiscal year (Shrawan-Asadh) for seamless accounting and compliance.
