FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Providhy.

Do I need to buy a server or any hardware?

No, Providhy is 100% cloud-based. We handle all IT infrastructure, servers, security updates, and backups for you at no extra cost. All you need is a computer or smartphone with an internet connection to get started.

Is Providhy compliant with Nepal Tax Laws?

Yes, absolutely! Providhy is fully IRD compliant. It generates VAT Tax Invoices, Abbreviated Tax Invoices, Annex 5, Annex 8, and all other mandatory reports as per the Inland Revenue Department's regulations. Stay audit-ready at all times.

What types of businesses can use Providhy?

Providhy is ideal for trading businesses, retail shops, wholesalers, distributors, service-based companies, and B2B organizations. Whether you're a small kirana store or a multi-branch enterprise, Providhy scales with your needs.

Can I access Providhy from my mobile phone?

Yes! Providhy works beautifully on smartphones and tablets. You can check reports, approve invoices, and monitor your business from anywhere. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA), so you can even install it like a native app.

What happens if my internet goes down?

No worries! Providhy works offline too. You can continue billing and managing sales even without internet. All your data syncs automatically once the connection is restored — zero data loss guaranteed.

How do I get support if I face any issues?

We offer 365-day support via phone, WhatsApp, and email. Our local Nepali team responds in minutes, not days. Whether it's a quick question or detailed training, we're always here to help — even on holidays!
