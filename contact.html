Quick Answers

Common Questions

Find quick answers to frequently asked questions.

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2 hours during business hours. For urgent support issues, we typically respond within 5 minutes. Our support team is available 365 days a year.
Can I schedule a personalized demo?
Absolutely! We offer free personalized demos tailored to your business type. Simply fill out the contact form above and select "Request a Demo" as your inquiry type. We'll schedule a convenient time for you.
Do you offer on-site visits for enterprise clients?
Yes! For enterprise clients, we offer on-site consultations, training sessions, and implementation support. This is available for clients in the Kathmandu Valley and can be arranged for other locations as well.
What languages does your support team speak?
Our team is fluent in both Nepali and English. Feel free to communicate in whichever language you're most comfortable with. We want to make sure you get the help you need without any language barriers.
