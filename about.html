Our Story

From Frustration to Innovation

Providhy was born out of a simple observation: Nepali trading businesses deserved better tools. As entrepreneurs ourselves, we experienced firsthand the challenges of managing inventory, generating VAT-compliant invoices, and keeping track of finances with outdated software.

In 2022, we set out to build something different—a modern, cloud-based ERP system designed specifically for the Nepali market. We understood the unique challenges: the need for Nepali calendar support, IRD-compliant billing, and software that works reliably even with inconsistent internet connectivity.

Today, Providhy powers hundreds of businesses across Nepal, from small shops in Pokhara to large trading companies in Kathmandu. But we're just getting started.

Our Journey

Milestones Along the Way

From a small idea to Nepal's growing ERP solution

2022 Q1

The Beginning

Providhy was founded with a vision to modernize business management in Nepal. Started with a small team of 3 developers and a clear mission.

2022 Q3

Beta Launch

Launched beta version with 10 pilot customers. Gathered invaluable feedback that shaped the product into what it is today.

2023 Q1

Official Launch

Publicly launched Providhy ERP with full VAT compliance and Nepali calendar support. Reached 100 customers within 3 months.

2023 Q4

Rapid Growth

Expanded team to 15 members. Launched mobile app and advanced inventory features. Crossed 300 active business customers.

2024 Present

Scaling New Heights

500+ active customers across 25+ districts. Launched advanced analytics, multi-branch support, and API integrations. Preparing for international expansion.

2025+ The Future

What's Next?

AI-powered insights, expanded integrations, regional expansion, and continuing our mission to empower every Nepali business with world-class tools.
